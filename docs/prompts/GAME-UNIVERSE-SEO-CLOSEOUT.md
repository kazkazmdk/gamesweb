### Universe

**Japanese mountain-industrial touge at 02:00 after rain.**

Not generic cyberpunk.

a late-night illegal score attack connecting mountain hairpins, an industrial edge district, service areas and tunnels.

### World identity

Need actual places:

- wet mountain road,
- retaining walls,
- guardrails,
- forest edges,

- sodium-lit tunnel,
- industrial harbor section,
- occasional vending/convenience-light color,

- deep navy,
- concrete gray,
- forest black-green.

Must visibly separate:

- wet asphalt,
- painted road markings,
- concrete,
- steel barriers,
- glass,
- vegetation,
- reflective signage,

### Gameplay spectacle

Drifting must visually transform the screen:

- tire marks,

# 12. /play/[slug] treatment

The playable route must not compete against the canonical Game Hub.

- do not include play routes in sitemap;
- provide a visible/internal route back to the canonical game hub;
- keep game hubs self-canonical.

Do not rely on robots.txt to solve duplicate indexing.
